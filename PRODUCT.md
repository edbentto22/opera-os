# Product — Catalise.me

## Register
brand (the design is the product; not a standard template)

## Users
Business owners, operations managers, and company directors looking to integrate AI and structure their business operations into a unified system. They value precision, clarity, and automation.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the landing page and conversion hub for **OPERA OS**, an all-in-one AI operating system for service-based businesses. It showcases our integrated approach:
1. **AI Agents (WhatsApp, RAG, Lead Gen)**
2. **Operations & CRM integration**
3. **Dashboards & Metrics tracking**

The core conversion goal is requesting a **free 90-minute operational diagnosis** via an interactive popup form, which registers leads through a webhook.

## Brand Personality
Precise, technical, direct, systemic, and confident. 

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS templates with round cards, soft gradients, and bloated graphics.
- Over-promising marketing copy with vague terms.
- Unstructured layouts.

## Design Principles
1. **The System is the Product:** Layouts should feel organized, clean, and grid-like, reminiscent of an OS interface.
2. **Technical Accentuation:** Off-white backgrounds and dark charcoal text dominate. Acid green (`#6b9e11`) is used strictly for accentuation, status, and conversion.
3. **Zero Border Radius:** Strict flat corners across all components (buttons, cards, inputs) to reinforce a precise and technical brand language.
4. **Structured Motion:** Use exponential easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) for subtle reveals and interactive feedback. No cartoonish or elastic animation.

## Accessibility
Maintain high contrast standards (WCAG AA), proper focus states, and key controls (e.g. closing modals using `Escape`).
